Group FAQ

How does payment work?

One organizer pays once at the group link — quantity set to your headcount, minimum 15 at $5.99 each. Prefer a chapter invoice? Email us and we'll set it up.

What if someone's photos are blurry or dark?

We review every submission before generating. If someone's pics won't produce a great result, we email them directly with exactly what to retake — it doesn't hold up the rest of the group.

Do members all need the same background?

Up to you. For composites, have everyone pick the same color on the form (navy is the classic). Otherwise each member chooses their own — all the options are here.

What if fewer people fill out the form than we paid for?

We reconcile submissions against your paid headcount and tell the organizer who's missing, so you can nudge them — nobody's money disappears into the void.

How fast is it for a big group?

Same 3–5 business days once photos are in — members are processed as their forms arrive, not all at the end.
