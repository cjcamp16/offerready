FAQ

Do AI headshots look fake?

No — we start from your real photo and enhance it (lighting, background, sharpness). The result looks like you on your best day, not a synthetic avatar. Check the before/after at the top.

What photo should I send?

A recent one with decent lighting where your face is clearly visible. A phone selfie is fine. We'll guide you in the form.
