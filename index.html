Why it works

Recruiters spend seconds on a first impression. A sharp headshot, a clean résumé, and a real LinkedIn presence make you look like someone who has their act together — the materials do the talking before you ever get the interview.

Every order runs through a real AI pipeline — and then through a human. Photos are identity-checked against your originals, résumés are fact-checked against what you told us (nothing is ever invented), and nothing ships until it's been reviewed.

What we promise: better materials and a stronger first impression. What we don't: we can't promise you a job — nobody honestly can. We make sure you show up looking the part.

FAQ

Do AI headshots look fake?

Scroll up — those are real results from a real order. We don't slap a filter on your photo; we generate a brand-new studio portrait from your three photos, then verify it against your real face before it ever reaches you. If it doesn't look like you, it doesn't ship.

What photos do I send?

Three normal phone photos: one facing the camera, one from your left side, one from your right side. Shoulders up, decent lighting, face fully visible — no sunglasses or hats. The form walks you through it.

How fast is it?

3–5 business days from when you submit the form. Need it sooner? Rush delivery (1–3 business days) is +$5.

What if I don't love it? What's the refund policy?

Every order includes one free revision round within 7 days of delivery — one reply listing all the tweaks you want, turned around in ~2 business days. Before production starts, you can cancel for a full refund, no questions. After delivery, if something genuinely doesn't meet the standard you see on this page and a revision can't fix it, we refund that item. Full details in our Terms.

What exactly do I get, and can I use it anywhere?

Headshots as high-resolution PNGs (portrait-cropped, LinkedIn/print ready), résumés as two PDFs (a designed one for humans and a clean one for application portals), your LinkedIn banner as a PNG with copy-paste text, and your portfolio site live at its own address plus a zip of the files. Everything is yours — personal and commercial use, anywhere, forever.

What about glasses, hijab, braids, a beard?

Kept, exactly as you wear them — the AI changes your background and attire, not who you are. Use the form's styling notes for specifics (glasses on or off, keep the beard, etc.). We never alter your identity: no slimming, no skin-tone changes, no "beautifying."

Will employers care that it's AI?

These are brand-new portraits generated from your real photos and verified against your face — not filters and not anyone else's body. There's no watermark, and it's your photo to use like any studio shot. What recruiters notice is that you look sharp.

How long does my website stay online?

Hosting is included for at least 12 months — and in practice for as long as OfferReady exists; we don't delete client sites. You also get a zip of the whole site that works on any host, and if hosting ever wound down you'd get 30 days' notice. Details in the Terms.

Can I pick how everything looks?

Yes. The form lets you choose attire (suit, dress, or business casual), headshot background, a color theme, and overall style — and that one choice is applied consistently across your headshots, résumé, LinkedIn assets, and portfolio site.

Do I get to pick my website's address?

Yes — Full Launch Kit sites live at their own address, like jane-smith.offerreadynow.us. Pick the name in the form, and rename it anytime after delivery, free.

Is my photo private?

Yes. Your photos are used only to make your order, never shared, sold, or used in our marketing — and they're purged within 90 days of delivery (or the same day you ask). Full detail, including exactly which services touch your data, in our Privacy Policy.

How do group orders work?

One organizer (treasurer, exec board) pays once at the group link — 15+ people × $5.99. Then share our form link with your chapter: each member fills out a ~2-minute form with their own photos, background, and attire picks, plus your chapter name so we match them to your order. Everyone gets their own headshots by email. We reconcile submissions against the paid headcount, and email us first if you want an invoice or have questions.
